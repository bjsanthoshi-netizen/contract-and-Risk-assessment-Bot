COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT ("Lease") is executed on February 6, 2026, between Vertex Real Estate Pvt Ltd, a company registered in Maharashtra ("Landlord"), and StartupHub Ventures Pvt Ltd, a company registered in Maharashtra ("Tenant").

1. PROPERTY AND TENURE
The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant, and the Tenant hereby leases from the Landlord, the commercial office space located at:

Address: 5th Floor, Business Tower, Bandra, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400050
Built-up Area: 2,500 square feet (approximately)
Floor: 5th Floor
Term: 3 (Three) Years from the date of execution of this Lease

2. RENT AND OTHER CHARGES
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 1,25,000 (Rupees One Lakh Twenty-Five Thousand) per month payable within the first week of each calendar month. Additionally, the Tenant shall pay:

a) Maintenance and Common Area Charges: Rs. 15,000 per month
b) Property Tax: Rs. 20,000 per month
c) Electricity charges: As per meter reading, separate meter provided
d) Security Deposit: Rs. 3,75,000 (3 months' rent) deposited upfront

3. RENEWAL AND ESCALATION
The Lease shall automatically renew for successive periods of 1 year each from the expiration date unless either party gives 90 days' written notice of non-renewal. On renewal, the rent shall be escalated by 8% per annum.

4. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Tenant shall be responsible for all internal maintenance and repairs incurred during the lease term. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs and roof maintenance. The Tenant agrees to return the premises in good condition at the end of the lease.

5. DEFAULT AND PENALTIES
If Tenant fails to pay rent within 5 days of due date, a penalty of Rs. 5,000 shall be levied for each month of default. The Landlord reserves the right to impose additional charges of Rs. 1,000 per day for any overdue payment beyond 10 days.

6. TERMINATION CLAUSE - LANDLORD'S RIGHT
The Landlord may terminate this Lease immediately without any notice or cause assignment. Upon termination, the Tenant shall vacate the premises within 48 hours. The Landlord shall have the right to re-enter and take possession without recourse to legal proceedings.

7. INDEMNITY BY TENANT
The Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord from all liabilities, damages, claims, and losses arising from:
a) Any injury to persons or damage to property in the leased premises
b) Any violation of applicable laws or regulations
c) Any negligence or wrongful act of the Tenant
d) Environmental contamination or pollution caused by Tenant's activities

8. USE OF PREMISES
The Tenant shall use the premises solely as an office space and for software development activities. The Tenant shall not use the premises for any unlawful purposes, manufacturing, storage of hazardous materials, or any activity that creates nuisance.

9. LOCK-IN PERIOD
The Tenant cannot terminate or vacate the premises before the completion of the initial 3-year term. Early termination shall result in forfeiture of the entire security deposit and payment of 6 months' rent as penalty.

10. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY WAIVER
The Tenant shall maintain comprehensive insurance coverage for all movable property. The Landlord shall not be liable for any theft, loss, or damage to Tenant's property or person occurring in the premises. The Tenant waives all claims against the Landlord for any such loss or damage.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes arising from this Lease shall be subject to exclusive arbitration under the Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with seat of arbitration in Mumbai. The arbitration shall be conducted in English before a single arbitrator. The award shall be final and binding on both parties.

12. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by Indian laws and the regulations of the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM). The jurisdiction shall be with the courts in Mumbai only.

13. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
The Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet the premises without prior written consent of the Landlord. Any unauthorized assignment or subletting shall result in immediate termination of the Lease.

14. ENTRY AND INSPECTION
The Landlord reserves the right to enter the premises at any time without prior notice for inspection, maintenance, or emergency purposes. The Landlord may also enter for showing the premises to prospective tenants or buyers.

15. STATUTORY COMPLIANCE
The Tenant shall comply with all applicable laws including Labour Laws, Environmental Laws, and Building Bylaws. The Tenant shall obtain all necessary approvals and licenses required for its business operations.
